## 🌙 Atmosphere & Premise
*"Khang Khao Kin Kluai"* is a cheerful, well-known traditional Thai melody, yet within the ancient wooden home of a deceased Ranad master, it becomes an omen of dread.

Hired as the night watchman for the old estate, you arrive on a pitch-black night where almost all indoor lighting has failed. Guided only by faint moonlight and your flashlight, you must navigate hallways filled with the sound of fluttering wings and an endless, haunting Ranad performance echoing from the dark.

## 🧩 Chapters, Puzzles & Minigames

### 🔍 Chapter 4 · The Hidden Secret
* Outrun the ghost while collecting diamonds along the path, with selectable difficulty settings.

### 🌐 Chapter 5 · Multiplayer (Beta)
* Cooperative multiplayer mode allowing players to survive together.

### 🔓 Unlocking the Cell
* Locate the cell key to unlock the barred chamber.

### 🎼 The Eleventh Ranad Bar (Rhythm Minigame)
* **Pre-Game Instruction:**
  > "Press the keys in time with the Ranad melody to calm the spirit of the Ranad master."
* **During Performance:**
  > "Focus and listen closely to the melody of Khang Khao Kin Kluai. Do not miss a single note..."

### 🔢 Exit Door
* Input the 4-digit passcode found on the cases in numerical sequence (`1 → 4`).
