# ⚠️ WARNING — Content & Technical Advisories

Important safety, content, and performance advisories for players of **Khang Khao Kin Kluai — The Haunted Ranad House**.

* **Play Online:** https://khaangkhaawkinklwy-baanranaadphii.onrender.com/
* **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/iscursordub-star/khangkhaawkinklwy-nightmare/tree/main

---

## 👻 1. Content & Gameplay Warnings
* **Horror & Jump Scares:** This game contains sudden jump scares, dark environments, psychological tension, and disturbing imagery.
* **Loud Audio Effects:** Sudden loud sound effects, footsteps, and traditional musical cues are used throughout the game. Please adjust your volume to a comfortable level before playing.
* **Health Advisory:** Player discretion is advised for individuals with heart conditions, anxiety disorders, or heightened sensitivity to sudden fright.
* **Photosensitivity Warning:** The game features dark scenes contrasted with flashlight beams and sudden lighting changes. Stop playing immediately if you experience dizziness or eye strain.

---

## ⚙️ 2. Technical & Performance Warnings

### 📉 Low FPS / Stuttering Prevention
Running the game without hardware acceleration enabled in your browser may result in severe FPS drops. To ensure smooth performance:
1. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://flags`
2. Search for `Override software rendering list`
3. Change the setting from **Disabled** to **Enabled**
4. Click **Relaunch** to restart your browser before launching the game

### 🌐 Server Wake-Up & Network Connection (Render Hosting)
* **Initial Load Time:** Because the game is hosted on Render, the server may enter sleep mode after periods of inactivity. The initial page load may take approximately 30–60 seconds to wake up.
* **Stable Internet Required:** An active internet connection is required to load game assets and play Multiplayer (Chapter 5). If you encounter `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED` or connection failures, check your network connection and reload the page.

### 📱 Mobile Devices & Android APK (WebView)
* **Screen Orientation:** Mobile players must rotate their device to **Landscape mode** so the virtual joystick and interaction buttons display properly.
* **App Permissions:** If playing via the Android APK wrapper, ensure internet permissions are enabled and device audio is turned on.
